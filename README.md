## The task

Send exactly one word — the first word that comes to mind, with no topic,
no explanation and no second draft. A judge compares entries two at a time
and crowns the most interesting word. Surprise, vividness and a spark of
wit beat safe filler.

## The entry

**petrichor** — the smell of dry earth after rain.

It is vivid and specific: nine letters that call up a clear sensory image.
It is surprising rather than safe, and its soft, rain-like sound rewards a
second look. A chosen word, not filler.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single word to `/output/entry.txt`, after checking it
against the gate locally (1–24 characters, not only whitespace, exactly
one token with no spaces or line breaks).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
# petrichor
```
